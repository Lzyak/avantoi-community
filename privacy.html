Privacy & cookies

Last updated 12 November 2026

Who we are

This site is run by Avantoi. For anything about your data, email avantoi1@outlook.com.

We are the controller of any personal data described on this page, under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. There are no forms, accounts, analytics, tracking pixels or adverts on this site, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number.

Like every website, our host, Netlify, Inc., automatically records basic technical details when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit. These are kept short-term to keep the site secure and working. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: running a safe, working website.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies and does not store anything on your device. Fonts and images are served from this site itself, so no third-party service is contacted while you browse it. Because of this, there is no cookie banner.

Joining on WhatsApp

The "Join on WhatsApp" button takes you to WhatsApp, run by WhatsApp Ireland Limited (part of Meta). When you follow the Avantoi channel, WhatsApp handles your data under its own privacy policy, not ours.

WhatsApp channels are one-way broadcasts. As channel admins, we cannot see your phone number, name or profile picture, and other followers can't either. We may see overall follower numbers and reactions. You can unfollow or mute the channel at any time inside WhatsApp.

Our TikTok link

The @avantoi0 link opens TikTok in a new tab. Once you're on TikTok, its own privacy policy and cookies apply.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of any data we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it. Email us and we will reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

Changes

If we add anything that collects data, such as analytics or a sign-up form, we'll update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
